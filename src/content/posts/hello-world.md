# Welcome to my blog!

## Blog philosophy

We live in an era where anyone can post anything, and with AI its increasingly easier to do that. Writers could theoretically slop out topics they know decently enough to nothing about, post it on their substack, etc. I plan to do none of that: every word typed out here will be my own. Yes, I am an honest-to-god big-time clanker, meaning I do eat, sleep, dream and use a plentiful amount of AI in my own capacity, for work, for searching and reading, exploring, and playing with whatever new agentic framework exists.

However increasingly, I find a good respite from this automated dystopia in my own personal Notion knowledge repository, and also in writing stuffs i do for [NUS Computing AI Society's Corpus](https://aisoc-corpus.aisocietysoc.workers.dev), and hopefully here too. The idea is that while I may use AI to explore and partially exploit, writing everything by hand forces a reckoning with the content therein, almost like consciously sliding your finger across a book you read, or writing by pen and paper. (can you imagine that in todays era? my god. something physical that exists in the real world must be petrifying)

## Contents and topics

While I do plan to eventually work in tech, given its my field of study, and is where my passion and bulk of accumulated knowledge in the last 3 years exists, I may not write only about that. Other topics around things in the world could be fun too, lets see how I create the various topic abstractions to place them under. Generality is the name of the game here: while I love talking about technical topics in AI research and engineering, its almost a given that these do not exist in their own bubble, at least when applying it. And perhaps I'm yet to be proven by own experience, but I believe concepts across fields, not just within tech, can support each other in giving deeper insights.

The latter is what I want to focus on, insights. Where important, ask why, question things, and try to form relations. Writing something that most other posts don't cover, or at least providing a unique enoguh perspective according to my personal being.
